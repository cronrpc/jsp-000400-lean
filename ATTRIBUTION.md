# Mathematical sources and formalization contribution

The mathematical approximation theorem is due to Grigory A. Margulis.
The formal proof follows his 1989 work on indefinite quadratic forms and
unipotent flows. The lattice-reduction component uses the classical method of
A. K. Lenstra, H. W. Lenstra Jr. and L. Lovász. The original question is Erdős's
1961 I.34 in its positive-parameter, indefinite-form setting. Exact references
and the statement correspondence are given in [README.md](README.md).
